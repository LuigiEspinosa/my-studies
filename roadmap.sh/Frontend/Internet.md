# Internet

- The internet is a global network connecting computers and devices for information sharing. Enables browsing, email, streaming, and communication through interconnected systems. Acts as worldwide infrastructure facilitating access to online resources and services.
- The internet is a global network of computers connected to each other which communicate trough a standardized set of protocols.
- Information on the internet moves from one computer to another in the form of bits over various mediums, including Ethernet cables, fiber optic cables, and wireless signals.
- HTTP is the standard protocol by which webpages are transferred over the internet.
- Cryptography is what keeps our communication secure on the internet.
- Cybersecurity referse to the protective measures against criminal activity accomplished through using a network, technological devices, and the Internet.

A network is a group of computers or other devices which are connected to eachother.

> The internet is a network of networks.
