# Careers in Cyber

[Careers in Cyber (TryHackMe)](https://tryhackme.com/room/careersincyber)

Cyber security isn't just one thing. It spans more than 50 recognised roles, from penetration testers and SOC analysts to threat intelligence researchers, incident responders, security engineers and beyond.

Cyber security is one of the fastest growing career fields in the world and that growth shows no sign of slowing. AI[^1], cloud computing, connected healthcare, digital banking, and much more. New technologies create more roles.

[^1]: Artificial Intelligence is technology that enables computers and machines to simulate human behaviour, like learning and reasoning.
